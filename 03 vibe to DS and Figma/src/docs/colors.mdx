## Contrast notes

- White on `brand` (#ff6431) is about 3:1. That fails WCAG AA for normal text, and the brand `Badge` uses it. Use it for large or bold text only, or switch `brand-foreground` to `neutral-950`.
- `muted-foreground` on `muted` in light mode is about 4.3:1, just under AA for small text.
